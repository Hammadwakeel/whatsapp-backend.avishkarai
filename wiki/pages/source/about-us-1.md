## Summary
Inika Resorts is a luxury boutique resort nestled in the lush coffee plantations of Coorg, India. Derived from the Sanskrit word for "Little Earth," Inika offers a serene and sustainable escape from the hustle and bustle of everyday life. 

The resort's eco-friendly design seamlessly blends with the natural surroundings, providing guests with a sanctuary where they can slow down, reconnect with nature, and rediscover a sense of balance. From the scent of blooming coffee flowers to the gentle sounds of rustling leaves and birdsong, every detail at Inika Resort is infused with the quiet magic of the natural world.

Whether guests are seeking a romantic getaway, a rejuvenating retreat, or an adventure-filled escape, Inika Resort offers a luxurious yet sustainable escape where they can immerse themselves in the untouched beauty of Coorg. With amenities such as a swimming pool, recreation areas, and 24/7 security, Inika Resorts provides a truly unique and memorable experience for its guests.

A Sanctuary Called Little Earth

From the scent of blooming coffee flowers to the gentle rustle of leaves and the music of birdsong, every detail at Inika Resort is infused with the quiet magic of the natural world.
